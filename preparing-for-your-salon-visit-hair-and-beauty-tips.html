Preparing for Your Salon Visit: Hair and Beauty Tips

A little preparation can help you make the most of your appointment at Velvet Rose Studio. Whether you are booking a haircut, colour service or beauty treatment, clear communication and a few simple checks can make the visit more comfortable and help your stylist or therapist understand what you are looking for.

Before your appointment

Check the details of your booking, including the service selected and any preparation instructions provided by the salon. If you are unsure whether your appointment includes a consultation, or whether you need to arrive with freshly washed hair or clean skin, ask in advance. Preparation can differ between services, so it is best not to rely on a general rule.

For hair appointments, think about how you usually wear and care for your hair. Tell your stylist about recent colour or chemical treatments, previous at-home colour, and any changes in its condition. This information can help them discuss suitable options with you. If you have a particular look in mind, bring a few reference images and explain what you like about them. Be open about your usual styling routine and how much maintenance you are comfortable with.

For beauty treatments, mention any relevant allergies, sensitivities, skin concerns or previous reactions before the service begins. Follow any preparation advice given for your specific treatment, and contact the salon if something changes before your appointment.

At the salon

Arrive with enough time to check in and settle in without feeling rushed. If you are running late, contact the salon as soon as you can; available options will depend on the appointment and the salon’s arrangements.

Use the consultation to discuss your goals, preferences and any concerns. It is helpful to be specific: describe the result you want, what you would prefer to avoid, and how much time you want to spend maintaining the look at home. Ask questions if anything is unclear, including how the service will be carried out and what aftercare may be recommended.

Be realistic about what can be achieved in one visit. Your stylist or therapist can explain the options and help you agree on a plan that suits your hair, skin and preferences.

After your visit

Before leaving, ask about any care or maintenance advice relevant to your service. Make sure you understand the recommended products or routine, if any, and when to contact the salon with a question. A clear conversation before and after your treatment helps you leave feeling informed and confident about your next steps.
