Velvet Rose Studio Privacy Policy

Velvet Rose Studio is a beauty salon in England. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit our salon, book an appointment, contact us or use our website or booking services. We handle personal information in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Information we collect We may collect your name, contact details, appointment and service history, payment records, and communications with us. To provide treatments safely, we may also ask for relevant health information, such as allergies, skin or medical conditions, and treatment preferences. Please only provide information that is relevant to your treatment.

How we use your information We use your information to manage bookings, provide and personalise treatments, communicate with you, process payments, maintain business records, respond to enquiries and meet legal obligations. We use relevant health information to assess treatment suitability and protect your health and safety. We will ask for your explicit consent where required to process health information. You may withdraw consent, although this may mean we cannot safely provide a particular treatment.

Legal grounds for processing Depending on the purpose, we process personal information because it is necessary to provide a service or take steps at your request, to meet a legal obligation, for our legitimate business interests, or because you have given consent. We rely on your consent for direct marketing where the law requires it and for health information where applicable.

Sharing your information We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers who support our bookings, payment processing, communications or IT systems, and with authorities where required by law. These providers may only use information on our instructions and must protect it. If information is transferred outside the UK, we will use an appropriate legal safeguard where required.

Marketing We will send marketing messages only where permitted by law. You can opt out at any time by using the unsubscribe option in a message or contacting us. Opting out will not affect service-related messages about your appointments.

How long we keep information We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, including to provide services, maintain appropriate treatment and financial records, and meet legal requirements. When it is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

Your rights Subject to legal conditions and exceptions, you may request access to your personal information, ask us to correct or erase it, restrict or object to certain processing, or request a copy in a portable format. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. To exercise your rights, contact Velvet Rose Studio using the contact details provided when booking or at the salon.

You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113 if you have concerns about how we use your information. We encourage you to contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern.

Security and changes We use appropriate measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We may update this policy from time to time; the latest version will be made available by Velvet Rose Studio.
